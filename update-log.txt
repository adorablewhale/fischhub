fischhub 2.7.2
- loading is now served from adorablewhale.world after you accept the agreement and keep reporting on. the loadstring is the same one you already run.
- a new version gives you 24 hours. after that an old loader stops and tells you to rerun the loadstring to get the current one.
- diagnostics are off by default and you choose each one in settings: error reports, executor and roblox version, performance counters. the feedback box sends only what you type when you press send.
- an account the owner blocked gets the reason written for it instead of the script.
- live dashboard sync is every 15 seconds now (was 5), to keep the website calm.
